# Research history

This chronology distinguishes physical samples, theory calculations and
retrospective analysis. All dates refer to the local records; none is an
independent timestamp.

| Period | Work and outcome |
|---|---|
| 22–23 October 2026 | Admission and baseline work compared local rules, transport statistics, terminal wiring and prior literature. Adjacent terminals displayed contact sensitivity. A retroreflecting-return classification error was corrected and separately checked before confirmation; the crossing count itself was unaffected. |
| 23 October | Fixed the original analysis rules and sample allocation locally. An independent two-dimensional checkpoint passed only at its explicitly limited precision. Derived the mixed-twist covariance target under smooth-saddle and wall assumptions. |
| 23–28 October | Completed the original 10,446,000-environment collection, preserving interrupted output and resuming only unused allocated seeds. Second-cumulant and identity results included fit failures and five unresolved identity tests. |
| 28 October | Completed the fixed 18,167,000-environment addition. Retroreflection rejected the original 2/3 null. Third cumulants gave several consistent results but included an underpowered geometry and a wall fit failure. All fourth-cumulant tests remained inconclusive. |
| 28–30 October | The exact pure-retroreflection reduction motivated a retrospective partial-port explanation. Reconstructed full-face counts approached 2/3; this reused existing samples. Conditional geometric theory supplied a two-stripe target near 0.8163723. |
| 30 October | An independent 2,522,250-environment layout test rejected zero four-minus-one-stripe difference and was consistent with the predicted contrast. Older standard-rule absolute ratios had an upward offset, retained in subsequent reports. |
| 30–31 October | A fresh 1,146,500-environment absolute-ratio test at larger sizes retained both rules and both extrapolations. All three half-open ratios passed the stated consistency checks. Two extrapolations still differed appreciably. Auxiliary third cumulants and spectra were registered during collection and did not receive new primary status. |
| 31 October | Compared the candidate saddle directly to established multiterminal conductor theory. The general counting-source structure and variance functional were reclassified as inherited. The contribution was narrowed to the specific boundary reduction, geometry calculation and numerical applicability. |
| 31 October | Collected 128,000 diagnostic environments and a later fixed 68,000-environment holdout for finite-range response predictions. Holdout tests did not reject, but range sensitivity exceeded the desired physical-error scale. Quarter-open absolute ratios were about 0.5% high; independent single-stripe third-cumulant estimates were on opposite sides of theory. |
| 31 October | Added complete-distance output responses and specified fourth-/sixth-order source and two-loop matching calculations. A seven-parameter subset was full rank, but no complete physical remainder bound was obtained. All 264 candidate predictions remained unqualified for the proposed later confirmation, which was never frozen or sampled. |
| 1 November | User stopped further research and requested public-release preparation. A new literature check identified two-dimensional selected-boundary connection counting by Morin-Duchesne and Jacobsen. Preparation paused under the project procedure, then resumed with the user's explicit approval of the narrower novelty scope. No numerical verdict was changed. |
| 1 November | Independently checked the stripe closed expression; prepared this report, all original decisions and both extrapolations; rebuilt the primary analyses from all compact observations in a new environment and replayed four original blocks exactly. No new physical seeds or samples were introduced. |

Failed launches and superseded implementations are preserved in the private
project archive and do not replace or erase completed samples. That archive
is not included in this compact release. The public source manifest, seed
allocations and summaries identify the inputs supporting the reported results.
No external expert review or public timestamp is implied by this history.
