# Related work and claim boundaries

This is a scoped literature check, not an exhaustive priority proof.
Bibliographic records were checked against arXiv and publisher-deposited
Crossref metadata. The bibliography corrects Lee–Levitov–Yakovets author order,
Gruzberg–Ludwig–Read's first page (4524), and the authors of the Goldstone-phase
paper (Jacobsen, Read and Saleur). Nazarov's Generalized Ohm's law is cited
as the verified preprint rather than assigned the journal details of another
paper. An inadequately matched Levitov–Lesovik 1993 candidate is not cited.

| Work / reading scope | Relevance and limit |
|---|---|
| Sukhorukov–Loss, PRL 80, 4959 (1998); full text, characteristic-potential equations | General multiterminal noise functional is prior work. The partial-port variance formula is derived from it using the counting-variable dictionary. |
| Nazarov–Bagrets, PRL 88, 196801 (2002); full text, terminal Gram matrices and connector action | The counting-source saddle and its higher derivatives are an inherited framework. The mirror/conductor identity is formal near zero source, not an equality of microscopic ensembles. |
| Morin-Duchesne–Jacobsen, SciPost Phys. 4, 034 (2018), arXiv:1712.08657v3; sections 2.2, 2.6(f), 2.7 and 4.6, especially Eq. 2.41 | Gives a generating function for connections from a chosen boundary segment to its complement in a two-dimensional dense-loop model. Its normalized form is Z(tau)/Z(1). This directly precedes general partial-boundary loop counting. It differs from the current left-to-right-subset count and its three-dimensional stripe evaluation. |
| Akkermans–Bodineau–Derrida–Shpielberg, EPL 103, 20001 (2013); sections 2–4 | Higher-dimensional shape universality for two-reservoir stochastic diffusive current statistics under the specified macroscopic-contact and stationary assumptions. The present random variable is a quenched-environment count. |
| Gu–Schaeffer–Hao, arXiv:2609.23244v1; sections 3.1–3.3 | Fixed-aperture rerouting and nearby transmitting-port suppression in an oriented K-lattice have direct conceptual overlap with boundary spatial correlations. The current work does not claim that general observation as new. |
| Nahum–Serna–Somoza–Ortuño; Lefevere–Tasaki | Previously examined crossing-loop and mean–variance baselines. New version checks do not constitute a new independent proof review. |
| Nahum and collaborators (2011, 2013); Jacobsen–Read–Saleur; Serna | Loop/field-theory background. Relevant introductions and constructions were consulted; these references do not by themselves prove the continuum limit of the exact six-valent model used here. |
| Chiffaudel–Lefevere; Ruijgrok–Cohen; Beenakker–Büttiker; Nagaev; Blanter–Büttiker; Bodineau–Derrida; Bertini and collaborators | General transport, counting and fluctuation background; no paper-specific theorem is asserted beyond the scope read. |
| Gruzberg–Ludwig–Read; Beamond–Cardy–Chalker; Ortuño–Somoza–Chalker; Cardy | Previously read classical/quantum localization correspondences and their limitations. They do not justify identifying the present variance with quantum shot-noise fluctuations. |
