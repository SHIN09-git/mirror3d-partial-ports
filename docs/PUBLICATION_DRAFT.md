## Draft first-release text

This unreviewed research report studies crossing fluctuations under partial
boundary observation in a three-dimensional Lorentz mirror model.
Pure retroreflection admits an exact graph reduction. A stripe formula is
derived within established multiterminal diffusive counting theory and
tested with two local rules and two prescribed size extrapolations.

The package retains every environment in both primary stripe collections,
the complete original 30-test report, unfavorable fits and exploratory
quarter-open and third-cumulant discrepancies. Replays of both primary
analyses match the stored outputs exactly, as do four original seeded blocks.
A microscopic continuum limit and a controlled physical finite-size
remainder remain open.

JJIN HANZHE supplied directional guidance. Opus 5.5 proposed the initial
plan; GPT-6 Astra (max) performed later design, simulations, analysis,
derivations and writing. There has been no external human expert review.
The analysis plans were frozen locally, without third-party timestamps.
The general counting framework and earlier two-dimensional boundary
counting are explicitly credited.
